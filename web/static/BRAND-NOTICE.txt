VDM branding identifies this official Vast Development Method publication.
The wordmark/ assets isolate blue lettering from the originator-supplied
vast_development_method.jpg. The homepage composes them into one accessible,
transparent, single-line SVG wordmark: VAST DEVELOPMENT METHOD THEORY.
Existing letter shapes are reused; the R and Y extend the source construction.
There is no separately loaded font, no mosaic backdrop, and no detached caption.
The SVG is a layout for raster lettering; it is not represented as a vector font.

The small mark and favicon are derived from official VDM branding. The build
first requests https://www.vdm.io/VDM.png. When that endpoint rejects automated
acquisition, the official vast-development-method GitHub organization avatar is
an explicitly recorded alternative. The exact selected URL, source digest, and
acquisition notes are in assets/vendor/manifest.json and vendor.lock.json.
